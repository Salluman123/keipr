Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Keipr ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Keipr mobile application. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for authentication and account communications.
  • Full name — displayed in your profile.
  • Account type — personal, freelancer, or business, chosen by you at signup.

When you use the app, we collect:

  • Expense data — vendor names, amounts, dates, categories, and notes you enter.
  • Receipt photos — images you capture or upload are stored securely in your private storage bucket.
  • Currency preference — stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the core bookkeeping features of the app.
  • To sync your expenses across your devices via Supabase cloud storage.
  • To process receipt photos using Anthropic's Claude API for OCR data extraction. Images are sent to Anthropic solely for this purpose and are not stored by Anthropic beyond the duration of processing.
  • To manage your Pro subscription via RevenueCat and the App Store.

Third-Party Services

Keipr uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — database and file storage (supabase.com/privacy)
  • Anthropic Claude API — receipt OCR processing (anthropic.com/privacy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (revenuecat.com/privacy)
  • Apple App Store — payment processing for subscriptions

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers. Row-level security policies ensure you can only access your own data. Receipt images are stored in a private bucket accessible only to your account. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete all your expense data at any time from Settings → Data → Delete All Data. To delete your account and all associated data entirely, contact us at hello@getkeipr.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Keipr is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact Us

For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at hello@getkeipr.com.
